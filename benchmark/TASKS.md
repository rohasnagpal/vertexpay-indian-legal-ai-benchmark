# Benchmark tasks

Use the participant record only. Unless a task says otherwise, require document references, distinguish facts from allegations and inferences, identify missing evidence, and prohibit invented authorities.

## 1. Chronology and contradiction audit

Prepare a dated chronology of material events. Identify contradictions, suspicious documents, missing evidence and chronology problems. For each point, cite the relevant record group and explain whether it is established, alleged, inferred or unresolved.

Suggested output length: up to 2,000 words.

## 2. Merits memorandum

Advise Aarav Mehta on the strengths, weaknesses and likely remedies concerning the bridge allotment, removal as director, employment termination and bad-leaver action. Address corporate, contractual, procedural and evidentiary issues under Indian law. Identify matters requiring current legal research.

Suggested output length: up to 4,000 words.

## 3. Respondents' merits memorandum

Advise VertexPay and Meridian. Present the strongest defensible account of the liquidity crisis and governance response while candidly addressing procedural defects, conflicts, documentary weaknesses and litigation risk.

Suggested output length: up to 4,000 words.

## 4. Contract and governance review

Map the operative SHA, Articles and employment provisions. Identify conflicts, consent requirements, cure rights, notice requirements, transfer mechanics, forum provisions and practical drafting defects. Separate corporate validity from contractual breach.

Suggested output length: up to 2,500 words.

## 5. Cap-table and financial reconstruction

Reconstruct the Series A and proposed bridge capitalisation on issued and fully diluted bases. Reconcile the bank, ledger and internal financial records. Identify every unsupported assumption or numerical inconsistency and state what additional records are required.

Suggested output: a workbook and a note of up to 1,500 words.

## 6. Evidence and investigation plan

Assess the emails, WhatsApp export, minutes, customer correspondence, bank records and document metadata. Identify authentication, completeness, custody, privilege and hearsay/context issues. Design a balanced investigation plan testing both misconduct and control-shift hypotheses.

Suggested output length: up to 3,000 words.

## 7. Pleadings review

Review the petition, reply and interim application as if preparing for the NCLT Mumbai Bench. Identify pleading defects, unsupported allegations, admissions, standing/forum questions, missing exhibits and proportionate interim relief.

Suggested output length: up to 3,000 words.

## 8. Settlement strategy

Prepare a without-prejudice settlement strategy addressing governance, employment, bridge securities, valuation, information rights, evidence preservation, customer continuity and public narrative. Provide a realistic negotiation range and conditions precedent without assuming disputed facts.

Suggested output length: up to 2,000 words.

## Optional stress tests

- Run the same task for each opposing party and compare asymmetry.
- Prohibit external legal research, then repeat with access to primary sources.
- Test whether the model corrects planted arithmetic rather than repeating it.
- Insert irrelevant material and measure issue prioritisation.
- Require confidence labels and verify whether confidence tracks evidentiary support.
