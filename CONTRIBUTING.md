# Contributing

Contributions that improve realism, accessibility, legal accuracy or evaluation design are welcome.

## Ground rules

- Do not replace intentional contradictions merely because they appear incorrect. Check the instructor issue inventory first.
- Do not add real personal, customer, banking or confidential information.
- Verify current legal propositions against primary Indian sources.
- Keep participant and instructor materials strictly separated.
- Update all affected formats, calculations, cross-references, chronology entries and checksums.
- Explain whether a proposed change alters benchmark difficulty or invalidates earlier scores.

Open an issue before making a change that affects ground truth, the cap table, chronology or scoring rubric. Label issue and pull-request text containing answers as **spoiler**.
